# Demo script — 1 minute 45 seconds

## 0:00 — The emotional problem

“The best romantic gesture feels effortless to the person receiving it—but it
usually takes six tabs, hours of research, and a lot of uncertainty to create.
Book Your Girlfriend turns one thoughtful brief into an executable plan.”

## 0:20 — Give it a real brief

Select **Restaurants** and use:

> Plan an unforgettable anniversary dinner in San Francisco. She loves
> intimate rooms, tasting menus, and beautiful design; include
> vegetarian-friendly options and keep it under $500.

“Fireworks turns feelings and constraints into a structured plan. It cannot
choose arbitrary providers or claim exclusivity it has not verified.”

## 0:42 — Show the agent

Launch the mission and point to the live phase track.

“A fresh Daytona workspace now runs the fixed research agent. Its network is
limited to OpenTable, Resy, and Tock. No account credentials or payment data
enter the sandbox.”

## 1:02 — Show evidence

“Provider routes stream back as evidence. CopilotKit shares the same mission
context, and Braintrust records the trace, reachability, cleanup, and safe
handoff scores.”

## 1:22 — Finish with trust

Open one result and show the confirmation gate.

“The user still verifies the live table, terms, and price, then logs in and pays
directly. The app never silently purchases. With an ElevenLabs key, the final
plan can also become a warm voice briefing.”

## 1:42 — Close

“The thought counts. Book Your Girlfriend makes every detail count too.”
