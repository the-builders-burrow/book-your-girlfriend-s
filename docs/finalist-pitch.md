# Finalist pitch — 3 minutes

## Hook

Romantic planning is a high-emotion, fragmented transaction. People know the
feeling they want to create, but commerce sites ask them to already know the
restaurant, venue, gift, or destination.

Book Your Girlfriend reverses that. Describe her taste, the occasion, and your
constraints once. The platform turns that brief into a researched, observable,
safe handoff across the right providers.

## Product

The six modes are restaurants, private venues, gifts, experiences, getaways,
and “surprise me.” Fireworks creates a structured category-specific plan.
Daytona runs a new restricted agent workspace for the mission. Results stream
into a CopilotKit-aware interface, Braintrust captures the trace and safety
scores, and ElevenLabs can narrate the final reveal.

## Trust

Agentic commerce should not mean invisible commerce. The sandbox never receives
passwords or payment details. Provider IDs and hosts are fixed. “Reachable”
never means “available.” Every purchase ends at a human confirmation gate on the
provider’s own site.

## Why now

Models are good at translating emotional intent into constraints; sandboxes are
good at bounded execution; observability makes that execution legible. Together
they make concierge-quality research available without pretending an AI should
hold someone’s wallet.

## Expansion

Provider APIs can later deepen inventory, availability, gifting, and
reservation flows while preserving the same confirmation boundary. Preference
memory can evolve from each occasion into a private relationship concierge.
